Platform

Everything you need to run agents in production

  • Guardrails by default

    Every agent runs with scoped permissions, spending limits and a policy engine that blocks risky actions before they happen.

  • Human-in-the-loop approvals

    Route high-impact steps to the right person in Slack or email, with full context and one-click approve or reject.

  • Complete audit trail

    Every observation, decision and tool call is recorded and searchable, so compliance reviews take minutes, not weeks.

  • Connects to your stack

    Native connectors for Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP, Snowflake and 180+ systems, plus a typed SDK for everything else.

  • Evaluated before production

    Replay real historical cases against new agent versions and ship only when quality and safety scores improve.

  • Enterprise-grade security

    SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, SSO/SAML, customer-managed keys and regional data residency in the EU and US.

How it works

From first connection to full autonomy in four steps

  1. Connect

    Link your systems with read-only access first. Orbital maps your data and processes in a sandbox.

  2. Design

    Describe the workflow in plain language. Orbital proposes an agent, its tools and its guardrails for your review.

  3. Validate

    Run the agent against thousands of past cases. Review its decisions and approve the rollout plan.

  4. Operate

    Go live with gradual autonomy. Dashboards show throughput, quality and every escalation in real time.
